What We Do

Consulting built for the realities of an MSME

Kingfisher & Kite is a business process and software consulting firm for micro, small and medium enterprises. Most of our clients are owner-led. Decisions sit with one or two people, cash is tight, and the team is wearing many hats.

That is why we do not hand you a thick report and leave. We work alongside you to map how work really gets done, remove what slows it down, and set up the tools and numbers that let you decide faster. Our work falls into five areas:

  • Enterprise solutions consulting
  • Machine learning and automation
  • Operations management systems
  • Brand, sales and marketing development
  • Education technology and corporate training

How we think about it

Our Approach

Four ideas behind every engagement

Whatever the project, we come back to the same four questions. Pick one to see what it means in practice.

Engage Through Insights

Your sales records, customer list and supplier invoices already hold the answers to questions like which products earn the most, which customers come back, and which days are slow. We pull that data together so you can decide how, when and what to sell, instead of guessing.

Accelerate Business Growth

Growth stalls when every order needs the owner's approval, when stock is counted on paper, or when three people re-enter the same data. We find those steps, remove the ones that add no value, and speed up the rest.

Lead With Purpose

Before tools, we help you answer a simple question: what is this business for, and who is it for? A clear purpose makes it easier to say no to distractions and to act with confidence when conditions are uncertain.

Fuel Growth With Data-Driven Decisions

Once your numbers are reliable, we build a growth plan on them: where to open, what to launch, how much stock to carry, when to hire. The plan gets reviewed against actual results every month, so it stays honest.
