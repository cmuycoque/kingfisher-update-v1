Our Services

Four service lines, each tailored to your business. Here is what each one includes and what you walk away with.

01 · Consulting

MSME Solutions Consulting

This is where most clients begin. We review how your business makes money, where it spends, how work moves from order to delivery, and who makes which decisions. Then we turn the findings into a plain-language roadmap, ranked by impact and effort.

  • Business and process review
  • Pricing, margin and cost analysis
  • Growth roadmap with owners and deadlines

What you get

A written assessment, a prioritized list of fixes, and a 90 day action plan your team can follow without us.

02 · Operations

Operations Management Systems

For a restaurant, that means recipe costing, supplier ordering and a daily sales and cash report. For a retailer, it means stock counts, reorder points and cashier reconciliation. For a service company, it means job tracking and billing. We design the routine, set up the tool and train the staff.

  • Menu and product costing
  • Inventory and purchasing workflows
  • Daily cash and sales reporting
  • Staff roles, checklists and scheduling

What you get

Documented procedures, configured systems and trained staff, so the business runs the same way whether or not the owner is in.

03 · Analytics

Analytics and Insights

We connect the data you already have, from your point of sale, accounting files and online orders, and clean it so the numbers agree with each other. Then we build a small set of dashboards that answer the questions owners ask every week: what sold, what it cost, who bought, and what is changing.

  • Sales, margin and customer dashboards
  • Product and branch performance comparison
  • Monthly review with recommendations

What you get

A dashboard you can open on your phone, a short monthly note on what moved, and a team that knows how to read it.

04 · Automation

Automation and AI

We look for work that is repeated, rule based and prone to error, like copying orders between apps, sending invoices, chasing payments or compiling weekly reports, and automate it. Where you have enough history, we add forecasting for demand or stock, so you buy and staff for what is likely to happen.

  • Workflow and reporting automation
  • Demand and inventory forecasting
  • Customer reply and follow up assistants

What you get

Hours of manual work removed each week, fewer mistakes, and clear notes on how each automation works and who owns it.

Process

How we work with you

The same four steps apply whichever service you choose.

Discover

We learn your business, your numbers and your constraints through interviews and a walk through of daily work.

Design

We propose a solution sized to your budget and team, and agree on scope, timeline and fees in writing.

Deliver

We build and set it up with your people, in short cycles, so you see progress every week.

Grow

We review results against the targets we set, fix what is not working and plan the next improvement.
