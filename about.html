About Kingfisher & Kite

A consultancy that sits beside owners and teams, not above them.

Who We Are

Built for owner-led businesses

Kingfisher & Kite is a business process and software consulting firm focused on MSMEs. Our clients are the cafes, food producers, retailers and service companies that make up most of the Philippine economy, and that rarely get advice sized for them.

The name says how we work. The kingfisher dives with precision on a specific target. The kite rides the wind and goes higher. We pair sharp, specific fixes with a broader view of where your business is headed.

Our team combines operations, finance, marketing and software skills, so a single engagement can cover the process, the numbers and the tool that supports them.

Focus Areas

What we specialize in

Five areas, chosen because they are where small businesses most often get stuck.

Enterprise Solutions Consulting

Choosing, configuring and rolling out the core systems a growing company needs, such as accounting, inventory, point of sale and customer records, so they talk to each other.

Machine Learning and Automation

Automating repeat work and applying simple forecasting, for example predicting weekly demand so you buy the right amount of stock.

Operations Management Systems

Standard procedures, checklists and reports that make daily operations repeatable, even when the owner is away.

Brand, Sales and Marketing Development

Positioning, pricing, channels and promotions grounded in who your best customers are and what they actually buy.

Education Tech and Corporate Training

Hands-on training so your people can run the new process and tools without needing us in the room.

Customer Engagement

Loyalty, feedback and repeat purchase programs that turn one-time buyers into regulars you can reach directly.

Our Framework

Four pillars of growth

These pillars guide how we scope and sequence our work. Most engagements move through them in order, though some start at the pillar where the pain is greatest.

Pillar01

Engage Through Insights

We start by understanding your market and your own records: who buys, when, how much, and why they leave. The result is a short list of opportunities you have not tapped yet.

Pillar02

Accelerate Business Growth

We map your processes end to end and cut the steps that add delay without adding value, so the same team can handle more orders without burning out.

Pillar03

Lead With Purpose

We help owners define what the business stands for and where it is going, so every decision on pricing, hiring or expansion points the same direction.

Pillar04

Data-Driven Decisions

With clean numbers in place, we set targets, track them monthly and adjust. Growth becomes a plan you review, not a hope you carry.

How an Engagement Runs

What to expect when we work together

A short discovery

One or two conversations, a walk through your operation and a look at your records. We tell you what we see and whether we are the right fit.

A clear scope

You get a written plan with deliverables, timeline and fees before anything starts. No open-ended retainers unless you want one.

Handover, not dependence

We document what we build and train your team on it. Our goal is that you can run it yourselves, with us available if you need us.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What size of business do you work with?

Mostly owner-led businesses that are growing faster than their systems. If you are smaller and have a specific problem, we are happy to talk.

Do you only recommend software?

No. Often the fix is a clearer process or a better report, and software comes second. We only recommend a tool when it solves a problem you actually have.

Which industries do you serve?

Food and beverage, retail and distribution, payments and fintech, and education are where we have the most experience. The same ideas apply across other small business sectors.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, and we put a timeline in writing before we begin. A discovery is short, and implementation depends on how many processes and systems are involved.

How do we get started?

Send us an email or use the contact form. We will set up an introductory call to understand your situation and suggest next steps.
